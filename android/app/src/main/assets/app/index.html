    AI coach · optional

    Commands like today, week, limit and focus work without it, offline. For questions in your own words the coach uses Google Gemini with your own Gemini API key (aistudio.google.com → Get API key). When you ask one, a short summary of your stats (totals, top app names, goals; never private apps) and your message go to Google. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. The key stays on this phone.
